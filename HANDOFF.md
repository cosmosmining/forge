### Built
- **`formal/forge_formal.py`**: property checking on generated blocks with SymbiYosys.
  - structural check;
  - unbounded proof by k-induction;
  - covers required for non-vacuity;
  - verdicts PASS / FAIL / INCOMPLETE with sanitized, hashed evidence;
  - mutation analysis, where survivors are checked for equivalence;
  - RTL inventory and spec skeletons.
- **`conformance/forge_conformance.py`**: builds a pack that records results from evidence
  files and never re-simulates, then verifies it.
  - chain of custody by SHA-256;
  - bit-exact rule that a claim cannot relax;
  - derived values recomputed;
  - not-yet entries carry no number.
- **`conformance/leak_scan.py`**: no pack is written unless the scan has 0 findings.
- **`run_all.sh`**: regenerates everything. **`tests/`**: 46 tests.

### Verified here (kind: measured, stand-in blocks only)
- **Formal.** 4 stand-in blocks PASS:
  - SyncFIFO 8x4: 10/10 assertions, data path proven bit-exact for all values, 5/5 covers;
  - GrayEncoder, GrayDecoder and the two composed: bit-exact over all 2^8 inputs.
- **Mutation.**
  - FIFO: 38 of 38 non-equivalent mutants caught, plus 2 proven equivalent.
  - Gray: 30 of 30 for each of the three specs.
  - Control, the FIFO spec without its ordering property: 14 of 38. So the score depends on
    the properties.
- **Negative controls, in the tests:**

  - an unreachable cover gives FAIL;
  - a spec with no covers gives INCOMPLETE;
  - a non-inductive property gives INCOMPLETE, not PASS.
